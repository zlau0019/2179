The Big Picture

Public transport in Malaysia is largely operated by Prasarana Malaysia Berhad which is the parent company of MyRapid, the national public transport infrastructure company. Services spans from rail lines and bus networks across the Klang Valley, Penang, and Kuantan.

Monthly Ridership Trend (2021 - 2026)

Rail ridership is more than bus and continues to grow year on year.

Annual Mode Share (2021 - 2025)

Rail consistently accounts for the majority of total ridership each year, driven by the ever expanding MRT network.

Ridership by State (2025)

Prasarana/MyRapid services are heavily concentrated in the Klang Valley (Selangor & Kuala Lumpur). Grey states have no Prasarana/MyRapid operated services.

Data sources: MyRapid Prasarana's Ridership Reports (Prasarana, 2021 - 2026) & data.gov.my
