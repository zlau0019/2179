01

The Big Picture

Public transport in Malaysia is largely operated by Prasarana Malaysia Berhad which is the parent company of MyRapid, the national public transport infrastructure company. Services spans from rail lines and bus networks across the Klang Valley, Penang, and Kuantan.

02

The Rail Network

Rapid Rail operates five lines across the Klang Valley. The Kajang MRT and Kelana Jaya LRT are the busiest, while the Putrajaya MRT is the newest and fastest growing addition to the network.

Bus & Other Modes

While rail dominates ridership in the Klang Valley than other modes, bus services remain very important especially in Penang and Kuantan where there is no rail network. Bus ridership has grown steadily since the pandemic.

04

COVID-19 Impact & Recovery

Malaysia's lock down in 2021 brought ridership to its lowest recorded levels. By 2025, every rail line had more than doubled its pre vaccination ridership, which is one of the fastest transport recoveries in Southeast Asia. Not bad eh, Prasarana.
